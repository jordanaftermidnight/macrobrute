# MACROBRUTE project audit

Date: 2026-10-16
Branch: project-review-diagram-redesign
Method: five independent read-only reviews (r1 to r5) plus the doc-vs-firmware checker run on the real docs, merged here. Nothing was changed by the reviewers. Nothing is built yet; docs and firmware are design-stage.

## 1. Scope and decided facts

Scope: Pico pins and buses plus the LPC bridge UART (r1), power architecture (r2), analog and protection circuits plus DB-9 pin correctness (r3), expander scope and doc structure (r4), DB-9 pin budget and rear-panel fit (r5).

Decided facts (binding; docs that contradict them are findings):

- Split power. The Eurorack expander is powered only from the rack (CP1A). The MicroBrute-side breakout is powered only from the synth (TP70 +12 V, TP71 -12 V, TP72 GND). No supply rail crosses the DB-9 cable; only shared ground. DB-9 B pins 7/8 become free.
- Two DB-9 connectors (A = outputs, B = digital + CV inputs), mounted on the MicroBrute rear panel in the Arturia "A" logo area, to the left of Pitch/Gate. No DB-25.
- The expander gains a standalone LFO and Sample and Hold (rack-only, no synth cabling) in addition to slew limiter, clock I/O, firmware dividers and 4 aux outs. Noise, mult and attenuverter stay out.
- Owned parts only. Parts not on the owned list are flagged with an owned alternative.
- Back-feed protection: series resistors on the digital lines so an unpowered side is never parasitically powered.
- No hum: ground and shield strategy must be consistent and not rely on a series capacitor in the ground.
- firmware/pico/config.py is the canonical Pico pin source.

Conventions: CM = docs/MACROBRUTE_CONNECTION_MAP.md, BP = docs/MACROBRUTE_BUILD_PLAN.md, BOM = docs/hardware/MACROBRUTE_BOM.md, TPV = docs/hardware/MACROBRUTE_TEST_POINTS_VERIFIED.md, CR = schematics/CIRCUIT_REVIEW.md, PCB = schematics/breakout_pcb.md, SB = schematics/breakout_stripboard.md, LAY = schematics/breakout_layout.svg. "svg text" means the cite is text-element order, not a line number. Source column gives the report (r1 to r5) and its own finding ID. Anything marked UNVERIFIED was marked so by the reporting reviewer and is not upgraded here.

Counts: 17 Blockers (2.1), 76 Should fix (2.2), 24 Nice (2.3), 16 conflicts (section 3), 9 owner decisions (section 4). Raw checker output is in Appendix A.
